Requirements

Please prepare the necessary items prior to your scheduled morning appointment.

For School-Going Students

  • Child's Written Samples: Used exercise books and past papers (at least one sample per subject).
  • School Reports: Copies of the most recent school-based assessments or term/year-end reports.

If Not Currently in School

If the student is currently not enrolled in an institutional school program, these document requirements are waived. You may proceed directly to booking without school records.

Route Guidance Summary

  • Look for the distinctive black building near the main gate.
